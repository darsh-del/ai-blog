# What This Generator Targets: Categories & Keywords

Simple explanation first, full keyword list below.

## Two layers

Every article is built from two separate files, and they don't have the same amount of variety:

### Layer 1 — The 5 topics (`data/config/categories.json`)

This is the actual ceiling on how different two articles can be. There are only **5 categories**, and the "brand" list and "generic" list are literally identical:

1. River Rafting in Rishikesh
2. Things to Do in Rishikesh
3. Ganga Aarti in Rishikesh
4. Best Hotels and Hostels in Rishikesh
5. How to Reach Rishikesh

Every article generated is about one of these 5 things. No exceptions.

### Layer 2 — 933 keywords across 30 clusters (`data/config/keywords.json`)

This is genuinely well-built — organized by search intent (`head_terms`, `informational`, `commercial_investigation`, `transactional`, `long_tail`), with notes on why each cluster matters and which competitors it was sourced from. It's listed in full below.

### The catch

Keywords give an article its specific *wording* and SEO targeting. They don't give it a new *topic* — that still has to be one of the 5 categories above. You can generate 933 different keyword combinations and still only ever be writing "about hotels," "about rafting," etc. That's the direct, concrete reason the duplicate-content guard keeps rejecting articles once a category has been covered a few times: different keywords, same underlying topic.

**If you want more genuinely different articles, the keyword list doesn't need to grow — the 5-category list does.**
